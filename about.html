Abo turibo

Twizera ko ubushobozi bwa buri muntu bufite agaciro.

HurZa ni urubuga rugamije guhuza abantu bashaka akazi n'abantu cyangwa ibigo bakeneye abakozi, hashingiwe ku bumenyi n'ubushobozi umuntu afite.

🤝
Guhuza abantu n'amahirwe
Icyo dukora

Akazi ntikagomba kuba ak'abize amashuri gusa.

Hari abantu benshi bazi gutwara ibinyabiziga, kubaka, gusana, guhinga, gukora isuku, guteka n'ibindi.

Hari n'abafite ubunararibonye n'ubushobozi bwo gukora neza, nubwo baba badafite impamyabumenyi y'amashuri.

HurZa ishaka gufasha abo bantu kumenyekanisha ibyo bashoboye no kubona amahirwe yo gukora. Dufasha kandi abakoresha kubona abantu bashobora kubafasha mu mirimo yabo.

Ibyo duha agaciro

Indangagaciro zacu

Uko twifuza gufasha abantu no kubaka icyizere.

🌍

Amahirwe kuri bose

Kwizera ko buri muntu ashobora kugira icyo atanga, hatitawe gusa ku mashuri yize.

💪

Agaciro k'ubushobozi

Guha agaciro ubumenyi, ubunararibonye n'umurava mu kazi.

🤝

Icyizere n'ubunyangamugayo

Gushishikariza abakoresha n'abakozi kubahana no kumvikana ku kazi.

❤️

Korohereza buri wese

Gukora urubuga rworoshye, rukoreshwa mu Kinyarwanda kandi rwumvikana.

Intego yacu

Ubutumwa bwacu

Guhuza abashaka akazi n'abakeneye abakozi, no gufasha abantu kubona amahirwe ashingiye ku bushobozi n'ubumenyi bafite.

Icyerekezo cyacu

Aho twifuza kugera

Gutangira mu Rwanda, tugakora urubuga rwizewe kandi rworoheye abantu, hanyuma amahirwe akagera no mu bindi bihugu bya Afurika.

HurZa ni iyacu twese

Ubushobozi bwawe bufite agaciro.

Hamwe, twubake aho amahirwe y'akazi agera kuri benshi.
